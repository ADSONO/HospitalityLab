## Fire Safety

Until now, HospitalityLab mainly focused on television systems, satellite reception, networking, electrical infrastructure, automation and software development.

Today the project expanded to include fire safety technologies, which are an essential part of hospitality infrastructure.

The first pieces of equipment available for experimentation are:

- A BAES emergency lighting unit
- A Type 1 Fire Safety System (SSI)

The objective is not to build an operational installation but to better understand how fire detection, alarm and emergency lighting systems are designed and used in hotels, campings, restaurants and public buildings.

As with all other areas of HospitalityLab, the focus remains on learning, experimentation and documentation.

## Thoughts

HospitalityLab is gradually becoming a broader infrastructure laboratory rather than a collection of independent technical projects.

Each new category helps build a better understanding of the technologies commonly found behind the scenes of hospitality environments.
