Today

After a few weeks away from the project because of my final exams, it was finally time to get back to HospitalityLab.

During that time, the project continued to evolve behind the scenes.

What happened

Fire Safety

The fire safety section saw significant progress.

Several experiments were carried out on the Nugelec ECA 4008, including work on the CMSI, manual call points, smoke detection and alarm devices.

This new category is becoming an important part of HospitalityLab and complements the existing TV, networking, electrical and automation sections.

Network

The core of the future network infrastructure has now been assembled.

The first equipment has been installed and the overall architecture is slowly taking shape.

There is still a lot to configure, but the physical backbone of the network is now beginning to exist.

Software

Development also continued.

The frontend keeps improving while the backend and API slowly start to take shape.

The objective remains the same: build a complete hospitality platform around the physical infrastructure documented in this project.

YouTube

A YouTube channel dedicated to HospitalityLab was created to document the project in video form.

Unfortunately, shortly after its creation, the associated Google account was unexpectedly suspended.

The appeal process has been started and I hope the account will be restored soon.

Thoughts

HospitalityLab is slowly becoming more than a collection of experiments.

Television systems, networking, electrical infrastructure, automation, fire safety and software development are now progressing together toward the same goal: understanding and documenting the technologies behind hospitality environments.

Even though progress slowed during the exam period, the project kept moving forward.
