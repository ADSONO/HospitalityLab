# Fire Safety

Fire safety systems are a critical part of hospitality infrastructure.

Hotels, campings, restaurants and public buildings rely on fire detection, alarm and emergency lighting systems to protect occupants and ensure safe evacuation during emergencies.

This section of HospitalityLab documents the study, testing and understanding of fire safety technologies commonly found in hospitality environments.

## Topics

- Fire Detection Systems (SSI)
- Emergency Lighting (BAES)
- Fire Alarm Equipment
- Evacuation Systems
- Maintenance Procedures
- Technical Documentation

## Objectives

- Understand fire safety system architecture
- Learn how different SSI categories operate
- Study emergency lighting systems
- Explore maintenance and testing procedures
- Document equipment and experiments

## Disclaimer

HospitalityLab is a personal learning and documentation project.

Nothing documented in this repository should be considered professional fire safety advice, installation guidance or regulatory compliance documentation.

Fire safety systems protect lives and must always be designed, installed and maintained by qualified professionals according to applicable regulations.
