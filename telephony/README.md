# ☎️ Telephony

This section documents the telephony systems, equipment and experiments used in the HospitalityLab project.

The goal is to explore both modern IP telephony and professional hospitality telephony infrastructures, from simple SIP phones to complete PBX systems.

## Systems & Technologies

- FreePBX / Asterisk
- SIP & VoIP
- IP Phones
- SIP Trunks
- Extensions and call routing
- PBX systems
- Hotel telephony
- Legacy and professional telephony systems

## Equipment & Platforms

### FreePBX

Experiments and configurations around FreePBX and Asterisk, including extensions, trunks, routing and IP phone integration.

### Mitel

Mitel telephony equipment, PBX systems, phones and related experiments.

### Snom

Snom IP phones, configuration, provisioning and integration with FreePBX.

### Yealink

Yealink IP phones, SIP configuration and interoperability testing.

## Current Status

🚧 This section is actively being developed.

More equipment, configurations, documentation and experiments will be added as the HospitalityLab telephony infrastructure continues to grow.
